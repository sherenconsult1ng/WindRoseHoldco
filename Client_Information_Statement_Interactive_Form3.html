Definition of Beneficial Owner受益人定义

A Beneficial Owner is any individual who: (a) owns/controls ≥25% of issued share capital or voting rights; (b) exercises ultimate control over management; or (c) is a settlor/beneficiary with ≥25% interest in an underlying trust, or exercises ultimate effective control over such trust.受益人是指以下任何个人：(a) 直接或间接拥有/控制不少于25%的已发行股本或表决权；(b) 对管理层行使最终控制权；或 (c) 作为委托人/受益人拥有信托不少于25%的权益，或对信托行使最终有效控制权。
